# Code of Conduct

ARSNEL follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want learning Python here to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or ability.
Beginners are the whole point of this project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors and learners.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Explain jargon; no "just" or "obviously" when answering questions.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and contributions that
violate this code, and may ban repeat offenders. Report problems privately to the
maintainers through a GitHub security advisory or the contact listed in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled confidentially and fairly.
